Current Roadside Reality

The roadside struggle drivers face

Vehicle breakdowns in Sri Lanka shouldn't mean endless phone calls, uncertain waits, or feeling stranded.

Hard to find help

When a tyre goes flat or the engine stalls on an unfamiliar road, searching directories or asking passersby takes time you simply do not have.

Not sure the mechanic has the part

A mechanic might arrive after an hour only to realise they do not have the right alternator belt, battery size, or hose for your specific vehicle.

Safety worries when waiting alone

Waiting on an isolated stretch at night with loved ones or alone causes severe anxiety when no one knows where you are or who is coming to help.

Simple & Seamless

How FixMyRide works

Get back on the road in 4 easy, transparent steps designed for speed and peace of mind.

1

Report the breakdown

Select your vehicle type, specify the issue (battery, puncture, engine, or belt), and indicate if a specific part is needed.

2

Choose a mechanic nearby

View verified mechanics ranked by distance, customer ratings, skill, and confirmation that they have your needed part in stock.

3

Track the mechanic live

Watch your mechanic's live GPS location en route on the map, view the estimated arrival time, and share your trip with family.

4

Close the job with a code and photos

Inspect the mechanic's uploaded before-and-after repair photos and hand over your private 4-digit completion code to seal the job.

Powerful Capabilities

Key features built for confidence

Every tool you need to stay safe, get help fast, and ensure quality repairs.

Smart matching

Mechanics are ranked by proximity, verified rating, and vehicle specialization so you always get the right technician.

Parts availability search

Search for mechanics who already have your exact required spare part in their truck before dispatching.

Live mechanic tracking

Follow your mechanic's exact GPS path in real time with continuous arrival time updates.

Safety share

Send your live trip, breakdown coordinates, and mechanic identity details to a trusted contact with a single tap.

Completion code with photo proof

Review verified photographic proof of the repair before handing over your secure 4-digit code to conclude the job.

Emergency button

Instantly dial Sri Lanka police (119) or Suwa Seriya ambulance (1990) and broadcast your exact coordinates in dangerous situations.
