Simple & Seamless

How FixMyRide works

Get back on the road in 4 easy, transparent steps designed for speed and peace of mind.

1

Report the breakdown

Select your vehicle type, specify the issue (battery, puncture, engine, or belt), and indicate if a specific part is needed.

2

Choose a mechanic nearby

View verified mechanics ranked by distance, customer ratings, skill, and confirmation that they have your needed part in stock.

3

Track the mechanic live

Watch your mechanic's live GPS location en route on the map, view the estimated arrival time, and share your trip with family.

4

Close the job with a code and photos

Inspect the mechanic's uploaded before-and-after repair photos and hand over your private 4-digit completion code to seal the job.
