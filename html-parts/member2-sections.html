Current Roadside Reality

The roadside struggle drivers face

Vehicle breakdowns in Sri Lanka shouldn't mean endless phone calls, uncertain waits, or feeling stranded.

Hard to find help

When a tyre goes flat or the engine stalls on an unfamiliar road, searching directories or asking passersby takes time you simply do not have.

Not sure the mechanic has the part

A mechanic might arrive after an hour only to realise they do not have the right alternator belt, battery size, or hose for your specific vehicle.

Safety worries when waiting alone

Waiting on an isolated stretch at night with loved ones or alone causes severe anxiety when no one knows where you are or who is coming to help.
